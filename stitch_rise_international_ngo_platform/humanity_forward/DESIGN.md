## Brand & Style

This design system expresses authoritative global stewardship paired with immediate, human-centered warmth. Designed for international humanitarian aid and community enablement, the aesthetic communicates uncompromised institutional trust, transparent stewardship, and vibrant, life-changing hope.

The style unites **Modern Institutional Clarity** with **Refined Soft-Layered Tactility**:
- **Authoritative & Institutional**: Anchored by deep oceanic navies that convey permanence, rigorous governance, and global authority.
- **Vibrant & Optimistic**: Balanced by bright foliage greens and electric accents that spotlight community resilience, progress, and immediate impact.
- **Human-Centric & Approachable**: Generous scale, deeply softened structural containers (16px–20px), crystalline glass overlays, and airy spatial rhythms avoid sterile bureaucracy in favor of human dignity and clarity.
- **Mission-Driven Focus**: Visual weight is consistently dedicated to field proof, progress tracking, transparent fiscal metrics, and clear donation mechanics.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to meet strict WCAG AAA contrast ratios for body content while retaining dynamic visual hierarchy across dense data metrics and emotional storytelling.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Navy (`#0B2145`)**: The core brand color. Anchors authoritative headers, full-bleed hero stages, structural footers, and foundational navigation surfaces.
- **Secondary Navy (`#102E5C`)**: Used for contextual elevation on dark themes, active state layers within dark navigation, and supporting structural headers.
- **Action Green (`#16B866` & `#20C879`)**: The lifeblood and action color of the system. Dedicated to active calls-to-action, high-trust donation buttons, completed progress phases, and verified impact badges. `#20C879` serves as the tactile hover and active-light state.
- **Electric Blue (`#1479E8`)**: Informational emphasis, active step indicators, and tertiary interactivity.
- **Warm Amber (`#F4B51B`) & Coral (`#E94B5F`)**: Reserved strictly for programmatic urgency, regional relief flags, real-time alert modules, and funding thresholds.
- **Backgrounds & Surfaces (`#F7F9FC`, `#FFFFFF`)**: An ultra-clean cool canvas (`#F7F9FC`) keeps cards and functional modules distinct over crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces.
- **Typography & Structure (`#172033`, `#5D6878`, `#DDE4EC`)**: Text Primary (`#172033`) guarantees a contrast ratio greater than 11:1 against white surfaces. `#5D6878` handles secondary metadata, while `#DDE4EC` delivers architectural boundary definition without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural authority and confident headlines with **Inter** for long-form narrative readability and functional data density.

### Typographic Principles
- **Commanding Leadership**: Headline styles utilize tight tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and substantial optical weight (600–800) to project credibility on reports, editorial hero sections, and mission proclamations.
- **Narrative Accessibility**: Long-form body copy relies exclusively on **Inter** configured with generous line heights (`1.55` to `1.625`) to prevent eye fatigue during field report consumption.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy**: Category badges, verification tags, and operational statistics apply uppercase or heavy semi-bold Plus Jakarta Sans with outward tracking (`0.01em` to `0.04em`) to establish high scannability across dense data interfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point geometric scale and a flexible 12-column responsive grid framework, calibrated for storytelling, transparent reporting, and conversion paths.

### Layout Rhythm & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout, `1.5rem` (`margin`) outer canvas margins, and `1rem` column gutters (`gutter-sm`). Cards and actionable widgets collapse into full-width single-column stacks.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout, `3rem` (`margin-md`) outer margins, and `1.5rem` (`gutter`) column gutters. Donation widgets shift to contextual top anchors or sticky bottom sheets.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout with a maximum container boundary of `1280px` (or `1440px` for immersive dashboards), `5rem` (`margin-lg`) canvas padding, and `2rem` (`gutter-lg`) gutters. Allows asymmetric pairing of emotional field imagery beside live donation ledgers and progress trackers.

### Internal Density
Component spacing uses `space-md` (16px) for interior elements like input field padding, and `space-xl` (32px) to `space-2xl` (48px) for container padding on impact cards and multi-step modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean atmospheric depth rather than harsh drop shadows, combining multi-stop ambient light, crisp perimeter borders, and frosted translucent overlays.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: Pure background (`#F7F9FC`). Zero elevation. Used for background structures and foundational canvas partitions.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Cards & Lists)**: White surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px boundary stroke of `#DDE4EC` and an ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(11, 33, 69, 0.04), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(11, 33, 69, 0.06);`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Modules & Floats)**: High-trust cards, stat indicators, and donation amount selectors:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(11, 33, 69, 0.06), 0 12px 28px -6px rgba(11, 33, 69, 0.08);`
  Transitions seamlessly on hover by shifting `-2px` on the Y-axis.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Takeovers & Flyouts)**: Full transaction dialogs, field reports, and verification overlays:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(11, 33, 69, 0.08), 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 33, 69, 0.16);`

### Glassmorphism Overlays
For sticky navigation headers and floating media overlays, the system deploys a high-clarity frosted glass formula:
- **Surface**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` (or `rgba(11, 33, 69, 0.92)` in dark mode contexts)
- **Diffusion**: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(180%);`
- **Edge**: `border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(221, 228, 236, 0.8);`

## Shapes

The shape system is set to **Category 2 (Rounded)**, creating balanced, approachable, and human-friendly contours without compromising institutional authority.

### Radii Application
- **Micro Radii (`4px` - `6px`)**: Checkboxes, status dots, and fine structural borders.
- **Standard Radii (`8px` - `10px`)**: Input text containers, dropdown selectors, and small button tiers.
- **Card & Component Radii (`16px` - `20px`)**: Premium cards, impact story modular tiles, interactive donation enclosures, and callout banners.
- **Pill Radii (`9999px`)**: Interactive frequency selectors (One-time vs. Monthly toggles), verification chips, category tags, and floating statistic pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Donation / Impact)**: Background `#16B866`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`, border-radius `10px`. Vertical padding `14px`, horizontal `28px`. Hover shifts to `#20C879` with a subtle elevation increase. Focus ring: `3px solid rgba(22, 184, 102, 0.35)`.
- **Authoritative Secondary**: Background `#0B2145`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover shifts to `#102E5C`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent background, text `#0B2145`, 1.5px border `#DDE4EC`. Hover shifts background to `rgba(11, 33, 69, 0.04)`.

### Multi-Tier Donation Amount Selector
- **Frequency Tab Switcher**: Segmented pill shell with `#F7F9FC` background and `4px` internal padding. Active state features a `#FFFFFF` elevated pill with `0 2px 6px rgba(11, 33, 69, 0.08)` and primary navy typography.
- **Amount Tiles**: Grid of 16px rounded tiles. Default state: `#FFFFFF` surface with `1.5px` border `#DDE4EC`. Selected state: `#FFFFFF` surface with `2px` solid `#16B866`, tinted interior `rgba(22, 184, 102, 0.04)`, and a high-contrast check indicator. Custom amount input integrates currency affix smoothly inside the tile.

### Progress Trackers & Impact Meters
- **Track**: Height `8px` or `12px`, background `#DDE4EC`, radius `9999px`.
- **Fill**: Gradient from `#16B866` to `#20C879`, radius `9999px`.
- **Contextual Milestone Pins**: Floating statistical indicators floating above the bar displaying percentage fulfilled and total beneficiaries supported.

### Floating Statistic Pills
- **Architecture**: Pill container (`rounded-full`) using glassmorphism (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` backdrop blur `8px`), bound by a `1px` border of `#DDE4EC`.
- **Content Layout**: Inline flex with an icon badge (in `#1479E8` or `#16B866`), bold value in `Plus Jakarta Sans`, and descriptive muted label in `Inter`.

### Impact Story Cards
- **Structure**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `18px`, border `1px solid #DDE4EC`.
- **Header Asset**: 16:9 or 4:3 high-resolution photography featuring absolute-positioned category tags in the upper-left (pill-shaped, `#0B2145` text over translucent white or dynamic category tints).
- **Body Area**: `24px` padding, title in `headline-md`, supporting synopsis in `body-md` (`#5D6878`), accompanied by an inline metric footnote specifying precise community outcomes.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #DDE4EC`, radius `10px`, typography `body-md`. Focus transitions border to `#1479E8` with a `3px` aura (`rgba(20, 121, 232, 0.2)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Size `20px`. In active state, filled with `#16B866` displaying a white crisp check or radio pip. Accessible outline visible on keyboard navigation.

### High-Trust Badges
- Compact institutional endorsement seals and security indicators. Subtle monochromatic or dual-tone styling using `#0B2145` and `#16B866` with micro typography (`label-sm`) emphasizing third-party audits, charitable ratings, and financial stewardship transparency.
